Flood Season

Along the Altamaha, spring floods now arrive twice a year. Families who farmed the bottomland for generations are deciding whether to stay.

Where
Altamaha River basin, Georgia
When
March to May 2026
Published by
The Piedmont Observer
Frames
14 photographs

Essay header. Facts sit in a side column so the title and dek read like a magazine opener. "Published by" links to the original placement when there is one.

One frame per row, full container width, at the photo's native aspect ratio. Captions follow AP style: place and date first, then who and what.

Paired verticals break up the rhythm. Use them only when two frames talk to each other. They stack on phones.

Narrower inset for quieter frames gives the sequence a pace, like a page turn.

About this reporting

I photographed the Fryer family over eleven visits between March and May 2026, alongside reporting for The Piedmont Observer's series on rural flood risk. Flood data comes from USGS gauge records.
