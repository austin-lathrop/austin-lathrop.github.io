About

I'm a journalism student at the University of Georgia's Grady College of Journalism, minoring in public affairs, and I report on agriculture, rural communities and the environment for The Oglethorpe Echo and Grady Newsource.

Before reporting, I spent two summers in Washington, D.C., including an internship with the U.S. House Oversight and Accountability Committee. I know how hearings, rulemakings and public records work, and I bring that to local stories.

My stories start with the people closest to an issue, like ranchers, teachers and county staff, and back them up with federal data. I shoot my own photos and build my own charts.
