# How to update your portfolio

Your site lives at **https://austin-lathrop.github.io**. You update it in your web browser with Pages CMS. You never need Terminal or code. Every time you click **Save**, the live site updates in about a minute.

## Open the editor

1. Go to **app.pagescms.org** and sign in with GitHub.
2. Click the **austin-lathrop.github.io** repository.
3. The menu on the left has four areas: **Stories**, **Photo essays**, **Videos** and **Profile**.

## Add a new story

1. Click **Stories**, then **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in the boxes:
   - **Headline**: as it was published.
   - **Summary shown on the home page**: one or two sentences.
   - **Published in**: the outlet's name.
   - **Publication date**.
   - **Link to the original story**: copy the address from the outlet's site.
3. Paste the full story into **Story text**. Links to your sources come along when you paste.
4. Click **Save**.

New stories appear in date order automatically. You never have to reorder anything.

## Add photos to a story

- **Lead photo** (under the headline): upload it in **Lead photo**, then fill in its description, caption and credit.
- **Photos or charts inside the text**: under **More photos and charts**, click **Add**, upload the image, and set **Show this photo after paragraph number**. Count paragraphs from the top of the story. If you give a number past the end, the photo goes at the bottom.
- **Feature a story at the top of the home page**: switch on **Feature on home page**. The story needs a lead photo, and only the two newest featured stories show large.

Use only your own photos, or ones you have permission to use. Credit submitted photos as "Submitted photo".

## Fill in your profile

Click **Profile** to change:

- **Opening line**: the big sentence at the top.
- **Availability line**: leave it blank to hide it.
- **Portrait**: upload a head-and-shoulders photo. The space stays hidden until you add one.
- **CV (PDF)**: upload a public version without your phone number. The Download CV buttons appear once a file is there.
- **About paragraphs**, **Skills list** and **Published in** outlets.

## Photo essays and videos

These sections stay hidden on the site until you add your first one.

- **Photo essays → Add an entry.** Add a cover photo, then add each photo in order. Choose a **Layout** for each one:
  - **full**: across the page.
  - **pair**: two side by side. Use it on two photos in a row.
  - **inset**: smaller.
- **Videos → Add.** Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link and it plays on the page. Switch on **Feature as the large video** for your best one.

## If something looks wrong

- Changes take about a minute to appear. Refresh the page after that.
- If a change never appears, the site may have hit an error. Open **github.com/austin-lathrop/austin-lathrop.github.io/actions** to see the latest build, or ask Claude to look.
- Every save is kept in history, so any change can be undone.
